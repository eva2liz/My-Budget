Savings goals e.g. emergency fund, a car, a trip

Track money you're setting aside for something specific, separate from bills and debt. Use Add money on a goal each time you set some aside.

Recurring expenses e.g. rent, subscriptions

Recurring expenses are automatically logged once per month, on the day you set, next time you open the app. Default paycheck applies to the specific paycheck you pick — since each new paycheck is its own entry, you may need to repick it once that paycheck has passed. Day-to-day, the Paychecks tab is the easiest place to assign bills each pay period.

Active recurring expenses

Recurring income e.g. payday

Monthly recurring income logs automatically on the day you set. Every-2-weeks income (like a biweekly paycheck that lands on a different date each cycle, e.g. every other Friday) logs every 14 days starting from any date you know it landed on — pick a recent or upcoming payday, past or future ones are all calculated from it. Either way it shows on the Calendar tab in the color you pick, and previews in the Paycheck planner before it's even logged.

Active recurring income

Every income entry — recurring payday or one-time — highlights the whole day in the color you picked for it. Recurring bills show as labeled tags in their category color on the day they're due, with a ✓ once that occurrence is marked paid. Click any income or bill to open and edit it. One-time expenses aren't shown here — see the Paychecks tab for those.

Add income

Every income entry shows on the Calendar tab on its date, in the color you pick here, and becomes a paycheck you can assign bills against below. For a paycheck that repeats every month, set it up once on the Recurring tab instead — it'll preview here automatically before its day even arrives.

Unassigned income

Income with no bills assigned to it yet, right here as soon as you add it. The moment you assign a bill to it in the planner below, it drops off this list — it's still fully there on the Calendar tab and in your exports.

Add a one-time expense or payment

For bills that repeat every month, use the Recurring tab instead — this is for one-off purchases and payments. Picking a loan or credit card category here reveals a Charge/Payment field that keeps its balance on the Balances tab up to date.

Unassigned one-time expenses & payments

Right here as soon as you add it. Once you assign it to a paycheck in the planner below, it moves there and drops off this list — this is just what's still waiting.

Paycheck planner assign bills to the paycheck that covers them

For each paycheck below, assign the bills it needs to cover, check them off as you pay them, and see what's left over to spend. Paying more (or less) than the usual amount this time? Use Edit on that bill — for one not logged yet, use Log & edit amount to log it and adjust before it's due; either way, it only changes that one occurrence, not the recurring template's default. Paying one bill from two different paychecks? Use its Split button (here or in the list below) to divide it into two linked entries with the amount and paycheck you choose for each.

Add a category

The first 8 categories get their own chart color automatically, in a fixed accessible order (extra categories share a neutral gray by default) -- but you can always pick a different color for any category with the color swatch above, whether it's one of the first 8 or not. For a loan or credit card, enter what you currently owe and today's date — every charge or payment you log afterward in that category updates the running balance on the Balances tab.

Loan & credit card balances

Mark a category as a loan or credit card under Categories & Budgets to see it here. Log expenses in that category as a charge (adds to what you owe) or a payment (reduces it) — the balance below updates automatically.

Import from your bank

Most banks (including Bank of America) let you download recent transactions as a CSV from their website. Upload that file here to bulk-add them instead of typing each one in by hand. Nothing is sent anywhere — the file is only read in your browser.

Backup & export

Your data lives only in this browser. Export regularly to avoid losing it, or to move it to another device.

Reset

Permanently deletes all expenses, categories, and recurring items in this browser.
